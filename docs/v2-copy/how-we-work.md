## The arc

**Eyebrow:** The arc

**Heading:** Four stages, and then we are done

**Body:**
Every stage reads culture, operations, and human capacity at the same time. Pulling one lever and hoping the other two follow is the pattern we exist to break.

### 01 — Appraise (cobalt)
A structured read of all three Pillars at the same time. Not a survey and not a score. We surface where the current design and the intended direction have come apart, and what that gap is costing in speed.

### 02 — Prepare (navy)
Leaders get clear on what change they could credibly lead, and whether the organization is structurally capable of leading it. This is Phase Zero™: the work before the work.

### 03 — Align (purple)
We co-design the architecture. Decision rights, operating cadence, governance, and the human capacity that has to carry it. Culture, systems, and people designed together, not in sequence.

### 04 — Hand off (gold)
You take it from here. The build belongs to your team, with the capability and the judgment already inside the room. We stay reachable, not resident.

## Qualification

**Eyebrow:** Qualification

**Heading:** This work has conditions

**Body:**
Whole-organism design only works when all three dimensions are genuinely on the table. We would rather say so early than deliver a fraction of what is possible.

### Strong fit (teal)
- Senior leadership is at a real decision point, not a hypothetical one
- Someone with authority is in the room and stays in the room
- You are willing to look at culture, operations, and people together
- You want the capability to live inside your team when we leave

### Not a fit (raspberry)
- You need a single workshop to satisfy a training requirement
- The decision is already made and you need validation for it
- Only one dimension is on the table and the other two are off limits
- You are looking for a long-term embedded vendor
