## Where the name comes from

**Eyebrow:** Where the name comes from

**Heading:** The Stoics did not teach in a lecture hall. They taught on a painted porch.

**Body:**
The *stoa poikile* was a public colonnade in Athens, open on one side, structured on the other. People walked in mid-conversation and left with something they could use that afternoon. Rigorous thinking, out in the weather, where the actual work happens.

That is the posture we take into organizations. Not a framework handed down from the front of the room, but structured thinking applied in the open, with the people who have to live with the outcome.

## Five intentional shifts

**Eyebrow:** Five intentional shifts

**Heading:** What actually has to move

**Body:**
Each of these is a decision about market position, not a decision about operational tidiness. None of them can be made in one dimension alone.

| From (raspberry) | To (teal) | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reactive | Regenerative | From responding to a move someone else made, to originating the one others respond to. |
| Resistant | Resilient | From bracing against what is coming, to being structurally built to absorb and use it. |
| Adequate | Extraordinary | From good enough to keep pace, to a position competitors cannot easily copy. |
| Transaction | Transformation | From surface fixes that hold for a quarter, to structural design that holds for years. |
| Insulated | Integrated | From siloed functions solving their own piece, to three dimensions designed as one. |

## What we hold to be true (navy band)

**Eyebrow:** What we hold to be true

**Heading:** Four beliefs that shape every engagement

1. People do not resist change. They resist being changed. Authorship is the difference between the two.
2. Technology does not resolve organizational problems. It reveals them, faster and in front of more people.
3. Change is the external event. Transition is the human work. Success lives in the second one.
4. Culture, systems, and human capacity are one organism. Treating them as three projects is why the last three initiatives faded.
