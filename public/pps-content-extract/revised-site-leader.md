### Master Your Message
**Title:** Deliver Confident Communication.

**Content:** **You communicate everyday** – the way you talk to yourself, with others (at work, home, etc.), and even through what you don't say. **Communication is your billboard to the outside world**. What you broadcast has **impact** – make it count. Harness the **power** of your **voice** to deliver your message **confidently**, **clearly**, and **concisely**.

---

### Create Extraordinary Teams
**Title:** Develop Team Dynamics.

**Content:** In every area of your life, you **collaborate with a team**: your **work**, your **family**, your **friends**, and more. A team is any group of people with whom you "_do life_".

**Great teams start with great team members**. To create real **connection**, **collaboration**, and **success** with any of your "teams" requires **understanding** each person's "elemental" style. Disagreements shift from dysfunctional to **healthy** and **effective**. Dynamics pivot to focus on **alignment**, **adaptability**, and **trust**; discovering ways to **turn obstacles** or problems **into opportunities** and solutions – together.
