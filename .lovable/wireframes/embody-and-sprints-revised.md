## Guiding Principles (both pages)

1. **One job per page.** EMBODY = "Is this the right depth of partnership for us?" Sprints = "Is a 90-day Phase Zero engagement the right next step?"
2. **Stop re-teaching the framework.** P.A.T.H., Pillars, Phase Zero, ROI of architecture — summarize in 2-4 lines, link to the canonical page, move depth to a downloadable brief.
3. **Timeline > stage.** Phase cards stay, but reframed as "what to expect, week by week" — not a P.A.T.H. recap.
4. **One ™ per page** (on the page's primary concept), per brand rules.
5. **Lead magnet pattern:** every tier page offers one downloadable PDF that carries the deep content (Pillars white paper for EMBODY; Sprints Playbook for Sprints).
